# Silvopasture and Bird Diversity in Colombia

This repository contains the code and data associated with the first chapter of my PhD dissertation, examining the impact of silvopasture on bird taxonomic and functional diversity in fragmented landscapes of Colombia. The project aims to explore how local landcover and land-use impact bird diversity, and how these relationships are moderated by landscape and environmental factors.

## Chapter Overview

As part of the Sustainable Cattle Ranching (SCR) project, this research uses data from 500+ unique point count sites collected over a decade to model multi-species occupancy and abundance. The study seeks to:

- Understand how silvopasture, a system combining productive trees and shrubs with pastoral land, influences bird taxonomic and functional(?) diversity through time.
- Generate recommendations regarding the optimal amount and configuration of on-farm tree cover to maintain key ecosystem services. 
- Explore key hypotheses such as the 'dominance of beta diversity' and 'intermediate landscape complexity' hypotheses in the context of fragmented landscapes in Colombia

The goal is to provide actionable insights for biodiversity conservation in the context of sustainable beef production and habitat restoration efforts in Colombia.

## Methodology

- **Data**: 500+ point count sites collected over a decade.
- **Analysis**: Bayesian multi-species occupancy / abundance models, alpha and beta diversity, community composition, functional diversity metrics

## Pipeline

Run from the repository root (open `Ch1-ssp-birds.Rproj`). Survey tables, point locations and site covariates are read from the sibling repository `../Ssp-bird-data-wrangling/`, and point-level landscape metrics (`Event_covs_lsm.csv`) from `../Farm_diversity/`, where the land-cover extraction and landscape-metric scripts now live; scripts stop with a deliberate `stop()` before optional or export sections.

| Script | Purpose |
|----|----|
| `Scripts/01_Wrangling_ms.R` | Builds the multi-species `spOccupancy` data list |
| `Scripts/Correlation_vis.R` | Screens candidate predictors for collinearity |
| `Scripts/spOccupancy_ms.R` | Fits the stacked multi-species occupancy models (one row per location × survey period) and runs diagnostics |
| `Scripts/spOccupancy_temporal.R` | Reshapes the stacked data list into the multi-season format (one site per location, year-group × season periods) builds the occupancy covariates the list does not store (hinge-coded distance to the forest edge, forest cover within 300 m, silvopasture split by type, mean survey date) and the loadings starting values, and fits the model set (`svcTMsPGOcc` with a spatially varying intercept only, equivalent to `stMsPGOcc` but with range clipping; 2 spatial factors): the primary model (habitat by precipitation) and its distance, time, silvopasture-cover and split-silvopasture variants, plus the habitat-by-landscape-canopy-cover models and the habitat-conversion models (habitat by canopy cover within 1.5 km, by silvopasture within 300 m, area-based or canopy-weighted, and by rainfall, with forest cover within 300 m, a riparian-forest indicator or a squared cover term as variants), and the 278-species base model, an additive model with silvopasture split by type, canopy-weighted silvopasture and forest cover within 300 m and 1.5 km canopy cover by woody canopy height (`add_split`), on which the number of spatial factors is chosen, which read the 1.5 km ring cover and woody canopy height from `Derived/Excels/Scale_of_effect_fit_covs.csv` and the canopy-weighted silvopasture cover from `Derived/Excels/Ssp_canopy_by_survey.csv`; the configuration sets the species set (98 or all 278), the number of spatial factors, the anchor species and whether the loadings start from an earlier fit; models are named on the command line, or in `Rstudio_sections` when the script is sourced in RStudio |
| `Scripts/Silvopasture_canopy.R` | Canopy-weighted silvopasture cover within 300 m and within each point's 50 m disc (dispersed-tree stands weighted by their imagery tree cover `trees_perc`, live fences, intensive silvopasture and isolated-tree crowns at 100%, forage banks excluded) from `../Farm_diversity/`'s snapped Meta land-cover polygons, matched to the surveys as its landscape metrics are (checked against the area-based `ssp`), plus the number of digitized isolated trees within 300 m (from `../../Mentorship/Digitization_Mathilde/.../individual_trees_final`). Writes `Derived/Excels/Ssp_canopy_by_survey.csv` (read by the habitat-conversion fits, their scenarios and marginal-effects figures) and `Isolated_trees_by_location.csv`; figures in `Figures/Silvopasture_canopy/`. Run before the canopy-weighted fit. |
| `Scripts/Scale_of_effect.R` | Scale of effect of landscape canopy cover (in progress): extracts survey-year canopy cover in rings (inner 50 m excluded) from 150 m to 10 km around each point count, reusing `../Farm_diversity/`'s farm × year raster clips, to `Derived/Excels/Canopy_rings_by_location.csv`, and plots its variation, cross-radius correlation and collinearity with `Dist_forest` / `Habitat`; extracts canopy height in the same rings (and 20 km) from the national height maps (mean height of the woody canopy, cover-weighted, and share of the ring under canopy ≥ 10 / 15 m) to `Derived/Excels/Canopy_height_rings_by_location.csv`, with their collinearity with ring cover, `Dist_forest` and `Habitat` → `Figures/Scale_of_effect/Canopy_height_rings_collinearity.png`; then fits the primary model (hinge-coded distance to the forest edge, mean survey date as the time covariate) with each radius's ring cover added, plus the unchanged primary model as a baseline (fits named on the command line, saved to `Derived/Rdata/Model_output/Temporal/Scale_of_effect/`); finally compares radii from the saved fit summaries (WAIC relative to the baseline, community canopy coefficient) → `Derived/Excels/Scale_of_effect_radius_comparison.csv`, `Figures/Scale_of_effect/Scale_of_effect_radii.png`; fits ring cover by woody canopy height from 500 m to 5 km (`h<radius>`; `ha<radius>` with habitat and rainfall additive at full chain length) and compares them → `Derived/Excels/Scale_of_effect_height_comparison.csv`, `Figures/Scale_of_effect/Scale_of_effect_height_radii.png`, `Scale_of_effect_height_interaction.png`; writes each surveyed row's ring cover, woody canopy height and mean survey date at the cover-by-height radii to `Derived/Excels/Scale_of_effect_fit_covs.csv`, read by the habitat-context and habitat-conversion models in `spOccupancy_temporal.R` (fitted here until 2026-11-24, as `soe_<model>`) and by scripts that predict from them |
| `Scripts/Habitat_conversion.R` | Habitat-conversion scenarios at the surveyed locations from the habitat-conversion model with forest cover within 300 m and canopy-weighted silvopasture within 300 m (`full1500_f300_cw`, the 98-species fit made in `Scale_of_effect.R` before the model moved to `spOccupancy_temporal.R`; pasture converted to silvopasture adds canopy at the canopy density of a randomly drawn farm, per location and posterior draw, from `Ssp_canopy_by_survey.csv` and the farm IDs in `Isolated_trees_by_location.csv`; `f300` on the command line uses the area-based-silvopasture model, its CSVs tagged `_f300`, its figures in `Figures/Habitat_conversion/Area_ssp/`; `f300_rip` uses that model plus a riparian-forest indicator, its CSVs tagged `_f300_rip`, its figures in `Figures/Riparian/Habitat_conversion/`): pasture to silvopasture and back, either the pasture / silvopasture in the point's own 50 m disc or all of it within every point's 300 m buffer (land cover at 300 m from `../Farm_diversity/Derived/Excels/Event_covs_lsm.csv`, at 50 m from the `Lsm_df_*_10.29.26.xlsx` workbooks in `../Farm_diversity/Derived/Excels/Lsm/`), the buffer scenarios with the resulting change in canopy cover within 1.5 km (canopy gain per converted area from the 50 m discs of points at least 90% pasture or silvopasture, extracted once to `Derived/Excels/Canopy_disc50_by_location.csv`; ssp capped at the highest value observed at the point's habitat after conversion, canopy cover within its observed range); counterfactual occupancy from the fitted occupancy draws shifted on the logit scale, with expected richness before and after conversion and, for converted locations, the share of the richness and composition (Bray-Curtis) gap to the real locations of the new habitat that conversion closes, and an NMDS of the real location-periods' fitted occupancy, held fixed, with the converted location-periods added (`vegan::MDSaddpoints`, vegan ≥ 2.6-6), plus the overall change in landscape richness ignoring the sampling design (union of the three habitats' mean communities) → `Derived/Excels/Habitat_conversion_summary.csv`, `Habitat_conversion_species.csv`, `Habitat_conversion_locations.csv`, `Habitat_conversion_landscape.csv`, figures in `Figures/Habitat_conversion/` |
| `Scripts/Land_use_context.R` | Land-use context for the habitat-by-time interaction: forest area (canopy cover ≥ 30% of vegetation ≥ 5 m) within the dissolved 5 km buffer around the Meta points, from Hansen Global Forest Change v1.12 (2000 forest plus the undated 2000–2012 gain, minus annual loss, 2000–2024; layers kept as clips to a 20 km buffer around the Meta points in `../Geospatial_data/Environmental/Hansen_GFC_v1.12/`, downloaded and clipped if missing) and from the Potapov woody canopy cover and height maps in the survey years → `Derived/Excels/Forest_area_meta_5km.csv`, `Figures/Land_use_context/Forest_area_meta_5km.png`; annual woody vegetation change 2010–2024 (Potapov WVSC: removal, degradation, establishment, enhancement; clips to a 50 km buffer in `../Geospatial_data/Environmental/WVSC/`, downloaded and clipped if missing) → `Derived/Excels/Woody_change_meta_5km.csv`, `Figures/Land_use_context/Woody_change_meta_5km.png` |
| `Scripts/Migrant_occupancy.R` | Occupancy of Nearctic-Neotropical migrants in Meta during the wintering season (15 October to 31 March by default; command-line flags give the 24 September community season (`sep24`), species-specific single-species windows (`swin`) and fits without pasture (`nopast`)): single-species models (`stPGOcc`) for a focal migrant and a multi-species model of the 10 migrants (`svcTMsPGOcc`, or `tMsPGOcc` with a location random effect for the non-spatial variants), one fit per variant named on the command line (`sep24` starts the season on 24 September for the multi-species passage variants, the report's community model), saved to `Derived/Rdata/Model_output/Temporal/Single_species/`; rainfall and distance plots, and the scale of effect of landscape canopy cover (one fit per ring radius, using `Scale_of_effect.R`'s ring tables incl. its 20 km ring) compared by WAIC → `Derived/Excels/Migrant_ss_ring_comparison.csv`, `Migrant_ms_ring_spatial_loadings.csv`, figures in `Figures/Striata_ss/` |
| `Scripts/postHoc_ft.R`, `Scripts/NMDS.R`, `Scripts/Marginal_effects.R`, `Scripts/Plotting.R` | Trait analysis of species' silvopasture effects relative to pasture and to forest (summary for both contrasts saved to `Derived/Excels/postHoc_traits_<fit>.csv`), community composition (ordinates the posterior latent occupancy states, or the occupancy probabilities with `community_state <- "psi"`, which adds `_psi` to every cache, table and figure name; figures in `Figures/NMDS_meta/`; habitat grouped as pasture / silvopasture / forest or, for fits with silvopasture split by type, live fence / dispersed trees; species counted inside each habitat ellipse saved to `Derived/Excels/nmds_ellipse_counts_<model_tag>.csv`, the species outside all three re-counted against 2-SD ellipses to `Derived/Excels/nmds_ellipse_counts_outside_x2_<model_tag>.csv`, and every species counted against the 2-SD ellipses to `Derived/Excels/nmds_ellipse_counts_x2_<model_tag>.csv`, drawn in a supplementary 2-SD figure), marginal effects of the hinge-coded model set (figures in `Figures/Marginal_effects/`, one subfolder per model family: `Hinge_models/` for the hinge-coded set, `Scale_of_effect/` for the earlier scale-of-effect fits, `Habitat_conversion/` for the canopy-weighted habitat-conversion model the scenarios use; including the multi-panel interaction figures for precipitation and distance to the forest edge, time, and silvopasture cover within 300 m, plus habitat by landscape canopy cover within 1.5 km from the scale-of-effect fits, whose covariates come from `Derived/Excels/Scale_of_effect_fit_covs.csv`; habitat optionally split the same way, the split read from the site table) and the sampling map |
| `Scripts/qmd/Preliminary_results.qmd` | Preliminary results report (Word, plus PDF with `--to pdf`; rendered to `Derived/Scripts/qmd/`): reads the fits directly plus the marginal-effects and NMDS figures, the NMDS ellipse counts and the post-hoc trait summary, so run those three scripts on the same fit first |
| `Scripts/qmd/Migrant_report.qmd` | Migrant report (PDF and Word, rendered to `Derived/Scripts/qmd/`): sources `Migrant_occupancy.R` for its data (no fits run) and reads the saved fits and the ring comparison tables, and, for the supplementary tables of migrants outside Meta, the point counts, survey table and traits of `../Ssp-bird-data-wrangling/`; its supplementary sensitivity checks are a child document, `Scripts/qmd/_Migrant_report_sensitivity.qmd`, knitted in only when `show_sensitivity` is TRUE in the config chunk (FALSE for the collaborator version) |
| `Scripts/spOcc_fns.R` | Shared functions, sourced by the scripts above, including `fit_temporal()`, which fits and saves one multi-season model |
| `Scripts/DAGs.R`, `Scripts/qmd/Math.qmd` | Causal diagrams and model notation |

Data lists and fitted models are saved under `Derived/Rdata/`, split by data structure: `Model_input/Stacked/` and `Model_input/Temporal/` (data lists), `Model_output/Stacked/` and `Model_output/Temporal/` (fits).

`Scripts/_archive/` holds superseded scripts kept for reference. `Data/`, `Derived/`, `Figures/`, `Extra_scripts/` and `Docs/` (correspondence drafts) are local and not tracked.
